About Ashanti Maurice

I'm Ashanti Maurice, a trainer, wrestling coach, and amateur fighter based in Perth Amboy, NJ.

I help people get stronger, eat better, and show up ready, whether that's a fighter heading into camp or someone starting their first program. I also film, edit, and build websites, so gyms and small businesses can work with one person for training and content.

Credentials

  • ISSA certified personal trainer and nutritionist
  • Nearly five years of experience in nutrition and strength and conditioning
  • Assistant wrestling coach at Raritan High School (the Rockets) since 2023
  • Has wrestled since sixth grade: folkstyle through high school, plus freestyle and Greco-Roman experience, and trains live with college-level wrestlers
  • Trained in film and video production through the performing arts program at Howell High School
  • Contributed to the Spotify RISE campaign featuring Joji through Yours Truly Studios, as paid contract work
