From Jackson to Brick

Same Elite. New home. Bigger future.

After more than 20 years in Jackson, the entire Elite Wrestling operation has moved to the second floor of Superdome Sports in Brick, a 135,000 square foot sports facility. The coaches, the standards and the process are the same. What's new is more room, more programs, smaller classes and more one-on-one attention.

Current practice days stay in place while we settle in. New classes will be added as the schedule grows.

Summer camp

Four days of wrestling for boys and girls in kindergarten through 8th grade. Mornings include strength, running and agility. Then come two technique and live sessions, plus games and a daily lunch. The week ends with a beach workout and a day at Casino Pier in Seaside Heights. You don't need to be an Elite member to sign up.
